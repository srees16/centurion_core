 2. GAPS AND CHALLENGES (priority order)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1 Data       Free US data is survivorship-biased: yfinance drops delisted
               names, and nothing records past index membership.  A clean
               test needs point-in-time membership (e.g. S&P 500 since
               1996) and prices for names that left.  This is the hardest
               problem and decides everything after it (US2).
  2 Costs/tax  An India-resident investor pays costs no US model has:
               - USD/INR conversion both ways (about 0.25-1% a leg);
               - LRS limit (USD 250,000 a year) and TCS on remittances
                 above Rs 10 lakh a year (adjustable against tax; a cash
                 cost meanwhile);
               - 25% US withholding on dividends (credit in India);
               - Indian tax: 12.5% LTCG after 24 months, slab-rate STCG;
               - broker commissions and platform fees.
               Rates as of 2025-26; verify at US3.
  3 Execution  Manual only.  Suggestions must allow for a fill at the next
               US open, decided in India's evening, at the broker you
               choose.
  4 Engine     nse_engine is NSE-shaped.  Porting it needs a US loader,
               calendar, regime inputs (SPY, VIX), benchmarks and costs,
               behind an adapter that leaves the India book untouched.
  5 Evidence   Results reported in INR and USD.  Gates against SPY / QQQ
               total return, plus correlation with the India book: an
               uncorrelated mediocre Sharpe can beat a correlated great one.

 3. DECISIONS TAKEN (14 Oct)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Advisory only: no automated orders.  The book is researched,
    validated and paper-traded; after its gates pass, emails suggest buys
    and sells for you to place by hand.
  - Swing / positional only, never intraday (as for India).
  - Ind vs US, two hard differences (your rules, 16 Oct):
    - shares: US positions may be fractional; Indian ones are whole shares
      only;
    - execution: India runs paper books and automated orders through Kite;
      the US has neither (no automated orders, no broker paper trading).
      The US module advises, and you place every order.
    - options: possible for Ind stocks through Kite; not possible for US
      stocks (buying, selling or writing) from a non-US DriveWealth account
      (your fact, 16 Oct).  No US plan, sleeve or suggestion uses options.
    So the shared engine works in whole shares at a $10M notional, and the
    US email scales to your capital in dollars and fractional shares.
  - The research protocol applies (docs/research_protocol.md): pre-register
    before any number is read, Kaggle runs, every trial counts.  A new
    family with its own registry; 2026 is the sealed holdout.
  - A separate tracker (this file); the India tracker stays India-only.
  - Universe (your ask, 16 Oct; US-Y3 closed): the S&P 500 AND the
    Nasdaq-100, point-in-time.  The Nasdaq-100 adds the Nasdaq-listed names
    the S&P 500 lacks (29 in 2013, 15 today: foreign listings such as ASML,
    PDD; companies like Tesla before Dec 2020).  Nasdaq Composite as a whole
    (~3,000 names, thousands of delistings) is out of reach on free data.
  - Data from 2006 so the 2007-09 crash is in sample (your ask, 16 Oct):
    prices from 3 Jan 2006 (warm-up), results from 2007.  Proposed for US5:
    gates on 2013-25 as India's; 2007-25 with 2008 reported as a stress
    test, as India's R4 (US-Y7 and US5 settle it).
  - US data stays free (your call, 16 Oct; US-Y5 closed): Tiingo's free plan
    only.  If its ~500-symbols-a-month allowance runs out, the downloader
    stops cleanly (progress saved) and the rest resumes on 1 Nov.
  - Suggestions start now, before validation (your ask, 14 Oct): the
    advisory v0 (US-A1) emails the India deployed rules' picks on today's
    S&P 500, labelled unvalidated.  The queue below runs in parallel; the
    US6 verdict decides whether v0 continues, changes configuration or
    stops.  Never an automated order.
