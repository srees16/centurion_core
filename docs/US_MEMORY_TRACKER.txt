          - CAGR ~ rf + Sharpe x vol - vol^2/2.  In USD (rf ~4%), 35% with
            MaxDD <= 35% needs Sharpe ~1.5 at vol ~19% (drawdowns 1.8x vol,
            as the India book) or ~1.4 at vol ~23% (drawdowns held to 1.5x).

            Under LRS a resident may not trade on margin abroad (derivatives
            to verify, UG12) and options are impossible from a non-US
            account (your fact, 16 Oct), so a US leg must earn it through
            Sharpe alone.
          - Published long-only US large-cap systems rarely sustain Sharpe
            above ~1.0 (to verify in UG0).  The likelier route: the US book
            as an uncorrelated stream.  Two books at equal risk combine to
            Sharpe (S1 + S2) / sqrt(2 (1 + rho)): India 1.16 + US 1.0 is
            1.34 at rho 0.3 and 1.46 at rho 0.1.  In INR the US leg also
            gains when the rupee falls, often in Indian sell-offs.
          - So the plan tests the US book alone AND as part of the combined
            India + US book, in INR and USD.  US-Y7 asks which you hold it to.

 2. GAPS AND CHALLENGES (priority order)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1 Data       Free US data is survivorship-biased: yfinance drops delisted
               names, and nothing records past index membership.  A clean
               test needs point-in-time membership (e.g. S&P 500 since
               1996) and prices for names that left.  This is the hardest
               problem and decides everything after it (US2).
  2 Costs/tax  An India-resident investor pays costs no US model has:
               - USD/INR conversion both ways (measured at US3: 0.5-1.5%
                 a leg, plus ~0.1% GST);
               - LRS limit (USD 250,000 a year) and TCS on remittances
                 above Rs 10 lakh a year (adjustable against tax; a cash
                 cost meanwhile);
               - 25% US withholding on dividends (credit in India);
               - Indian tax: 12.5% LTCG after 24 months, slab-rate STCG;
               - broker commissions (0.15-0.3% a trade) and plan fees.
               Modelled 17 Oct (US3, us_engine/costs.py); rules checked
               (UG12).
  3 Execution  Manual only.  Suggestions must allow for a fill at the next
               US open, decided in India's evening, at the broker you
               choose.
  4 Engine     nse_engine is NSE-shaped.  Porting it needs a US loader,
               calendar, regime inputs (SPY, VIX), benchmarks and costs,
               behind an adapter that leaves the India book untouched.
  5 Evidence   Results reported in INR and USD.  Gates against SPY / QQQ
               total return, plus correlation with the India book: an
               uncorrelated mediocre Sharpe can beat a correlated great one.
