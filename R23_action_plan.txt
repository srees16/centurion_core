5. RESEARCH-BACKED INSIGHTS
================================================================================

  A. META-LABELING (AFML Ch.3, Lopez de Prado 2018):
     - Secondary classifier predicts if PRIMARY model's trade will profit
     - 20 features (forecast strength, vol, regime, RSI, breadth, OI, FII)
     - Filters 60-70% false signals through probability thresholding
     - KEY CAVEAT: Improvement requires walk-forward training, NOT look-ahead.
       In backtest, model must be re-trained at each point using only past data.
       The pre-trained model in data/meta_label_models/ uses ALL data → biased.
       MUST implement online walk-forward training in backtest for valid results.

  B. REGIME-ADAPTIVE SIGNAL WEIGHTING (Nystrup et al. 2017):
     - HMM-based regime detection + regime-conditional asset allocation
     - Improves Sharpe by 15-25% over static allocation
     - Already built as P4 in forecast_combiner — just not connected
     - Key: blend with static weights (50/50) to avoid regime detection lag errors

  C. KELLY CRITERION AND LEVERAGE (Thorp 1969, Carver 2015):
     - Full Kelly for SR=1.1: f* = SR/vol = 1.1/0.5 = 2.2x
     - Half Kelly (safer): 1.1x
     - Current effective leverage: ~1.7 (IDM) × 2.0 (max) ÷ adaptive = ~2.0-3.0x
     - Already AT or ABOVE half-Kelly → limited room to leverage up safely
     - Leverage >2.3x destroys SR (proven by O1 sensitivity data)

  D. MULTI-STRATEGY DIVERSIFICATION (Carver, Systematic Trading 2015):
     - SR_combined ≈ SR_single × sqrt(N) / sqrt(sum_correlations)
     - With 2 decorrelated timeframes (rho=0.3):
       SR_combined = SR × sqrt(2/(1+0.3)) = SR × 1.24
       1.127 × 1.24 = 1.40  (still below 1.5)
     - With 3 decorrelated sub-strategies:
       SR_combined = SR × sqrt(3/(1+2×0.3)) = SR × 1.37
       1.127 × 1.37 = 1.54  (MEETS TARGET)
     - Requires genuinely independent sub-strategies, not just different windows

  E. DEEP MOMENTUM NETWORKS (Lim/Zohren/Roberts, arXiv:1904.04912):
     - LSTM directly optimizes Sharpe as loss function
     - 2x improvement over traditional TSMOM on 88 futures contracts
     - Turnover regularization reduces transaction costs at run-time
     - HIGH POTENTIAL but HIGH OVERFIT RISK for small stock universe
